# Refunds and Credits

Northwind Cloud grants a refund window of 30 days from the date of each charge. Requests submitted inside this window receive a full refund to the original payment card, while amounts under 10 USD are issued as account credits that never expire. Annual plans follow the same rule, and the 30-day window restarts at every renewal date. Charges outside the window can still be converted into account credits as a goodwill gesture, capped at one credit per calendar year. The following are excluded from refunds: domain registration fees, one-time migration services, and usage overage on pay-as-you-go accounts. To request a refund, open the billing console, select the invoice, and choose Request refund. Approved card refunds arrive within 5 to 10 business days.
